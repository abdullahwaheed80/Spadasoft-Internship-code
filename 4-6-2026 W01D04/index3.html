Display: flex;

This is the initial property you apply to a parent element to make it a flex container. Its immediate children automatically become flexible elements.

Justify-content

This rules how space is distributed between and around flex items along the main horizontal axis. Common choices include space-between, center, or space-around.

Align-items

This controls how your flex items position themselves vertically inside the parent layout wrapper. It helps center elements cleanly without tricky margin math.
